Away from the topology and the code, I spend a fair amount of time under the hood of a **1973 Oldsmobile Cutlass Supreme**. This is the running log of that work — the guides I wish had existed when I started, written down as I go so the next person (or the next me, a year later) doesn't have to relearn everything from scratch.

So far it collects step-by-step write-ups and printable PDF references for the jobs that took the most trial and error: rear drum brakes, master-cylinder and radiator replacement, the Rochester Quadrajet carburetor, fuel-system service, accessory and fuse-box wiring, suspension greasing, spark plugs, VIN decoding, and a handful of smaller odds and ends.

If there's one hard-won lesson buried in there: before you take anything apart, **take a picture**. Especially the drum-brake springs.
